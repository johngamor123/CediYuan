WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Help & FAQ

Answers to common questions about CediYuan.

What is CediYuan?

CediYuan is a currency calculation and enquiry app that helps you estimate amounts between Ghana cedis and Chinese yuan.

Is the exchange rate live?

No. The rate in this version is an illustrative example. It is not connected to a live currency feed.

Why might the final amount be different?

Rates and transaction terms may change. Ask CediYuan to confirm the current rate and applicable terms before proceeding.

Can I exchange money inside the app?

No. This version calculates estimates and prepares enquiries. It does not process exchanges or payments.

Does a WhatsApp message confirm my transaction?

No. Sending a message only starts a conversation. You need direct confirmation of the rate and terms.

Do I need to create an account?

No. A customer account is not required in this version of CediYuan.

Can I use the calculator offline?

The calculator may work offline after the app files have been cached by a supported browser. WhatsApp enquiries require an internet connection.

How do I get help?

Open Contact & Support and use the WhatsApp button to send your question.

YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Information

A simple explanation of this app's current behaviour.

No customer account required

This version does not require you to register or sign in to use the calculator.

Conversion amounts

Amounts you enter are used by the calculator in your browser. This version does not send them to a CediYuan server for processing.

App preferences

Your theme, conversion direction, and decimal-place preferences may be stored in your browser's local storage on this device.

WhatsApp

If you choose to contact CediYuan, the app opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. Information you choose to send is handled through WhatsApp and is subject to its privacy practices.

Clearing saved settings

Use the reset option in Settings to restore preferences. Clearing browser data may also remove locally saved preferences.

This page describes the intended behaviour of this version. It is not a substitute for a complete privacy policy if the service later collects additional data.
